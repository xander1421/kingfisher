## What the tally means
Exactly two rows are genuinely novel, and they are the two the mission named as the wedge. Everything else is assembly under permissive licences, with three caveats that shape the plan:
1. **MORK's UNKNOWN licence** turns the fastest engine into a spec, and pushes the first milestone onto hyperon (which, conveniently, is the one that already builds for Android).
2. **The two copyleft elders (BOINC, Golem) hold the operational wisdom**, not the code — so the deliverables that matter from them are written specifications, which is what S6 and the Golem report are.
3. **One real gap surfaced that the mission's capability list did not name**: commit/reveal with a worker-bound seal (row 16). Without it, replication across untrusted devices can be gamed by echoing another device's hash. It is folded into `out/PORT_PLAN.md`.
